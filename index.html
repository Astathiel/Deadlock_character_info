Phase 1 • Laning (0:00 – 8:00) Waves & Guardians
  • • Secure & Deny Orbs: Shoot orange Soul orbs to claim and blue orbs to deny. Melee kills on Troopers automatically secure 100% of Souls instantly.
  • • Wave First, Camp Second: Never miss lane Troopers for early camps. Push your wave into the enemy Guardian, then clear 2:00 T1 camps or 3:00 Boxes.
  • • 5:00 Bridge Control: Shove lane at 4:45 to contest the 5:00 Bridge Buff and pressure the enemy Tier 1 Guardian.
Phase 2 • Mid Game (8:00 – 18:00) Urn, Rifts & Walkers
  • • 8:00 Jungle Power Spike: At 8m, Trooper Souls shift 100% into floating orbs, and Tier 3 Camps + Sinner's Sacrifice spawn. Carries rotate through jungle paths between waves.
  • • Soul Urn & Unstable Rifts (10:00+): Escort the Soul Urn for team-wide Souls + Ability Point, and capture Unstable Rifts on side-lane bridges to summon siege Rift Troopers.
  • • Unlock Flex Slots: Destroy enemy Walkers (Tier 2 towers) to unlock your team's extra Flex Item Slots.
Phase 3 • Late Game (18:00+) Mid-Boss & Patron
  • • Push Lanes First: Always shove at least 2 lanes past the river before starting Mid-Boss or sieging enemy high ground.
  • • Mid-Boss & Rejuvenator: Fight inside the central pit after getting picks (global roar alerts enemies at 50% HP). Heavy Melee the dropping crystal to claim Rejuvenator revive credits.
  • • High-Ground Siege: Use Rejuvenator / Rift Troopers to break Base Guardians, destroy both Shrines, and defeat the Patron in the pit.
🎯 Side Objectives & Map Mechanics Deep-Dive How to play around the Urn, Unstable Rift, Sinner's Sacrifice, Bridge Buffs & Flex Slots
⚱️ The Soul Urn 10:00 • Every 5m

Spawns on one outer side lane (Yellow or Green) and must be carried across the map to the drop-off altar on the opposite outer lane.

  • • Carrier Rules: Carrying grants bonus Sprint Speed, but silences abilities/items, disables shooting, and reveals you on the minimap.
  • • Rewards: Massive team-wide Soul bounty + +25% bonus Souls & +1 Ability Point for the deliverer.
  • • Comeback Mechanic: If a team trails by 8%+ net worth, the drop-off point moves closer to the trailing team's side.
🌀 The Unstable Rift 10:00–12:00 • Every 6–8m

A King-of-the-Hill objective that spawns on a side-lane bridge after a 25-second global announcement.

  • • How to Capture: Stand inside the 20m bridge zone for ~12 seconds uncontested (progress locks at 99% while contested; despawns after 60s).
  • • Rift Troopers: Capturing grants team Souls and spawns Rift Troopers (buffed siege super-creeps) that march down lane to melt Walkers.
  • • Comeback Bonus: Trailing teams capture faster (down to 6s) and gain extra resistances inside the zone.
🎰 Sinner's Sacrifice & Boxes 8:00 (Machines) • 3:00 (Boxes)

Arcade machines Located in jungle rooms that trade a small amount of self-damage for Souls and permanent character stat upgrades.

  • • Heavy Melee Jackpot: Punch the machine until the indicator lights climb—time a charged Heavy Melee (Hold Q) right as the final top bulb lights up to drop 3–4 permanent Golden Statue buffs!
  • • Golden Statues (3:00): Smash wooden boxes & golden idols along rooftops and subways for permanent +Weapon Damage, +Spirit, +Max HP, +Ammo, or +CDR.
⚡ Bridge Buffs & Teleporters 5:00 • Every 5m

Two powerup runes spawn atop the central lane overpass bridges at 5:00 and scale in strength every 5 minutes:

  • • Gun / Spirit Buffs: Grants huge Fire Rate + Ammo (Gun) or bonus Spirit Power + Cooldown Reduction (Spirit).
  • • Survival / Movement Buffs: Grants bonus Max HP + Regen (Survival) or extra Stamina + Sprint Speed (Movement).
  • • Subway Teleporters: Use underground portals to instantly rotate between outer side lanes (`Yellow ↔ Green`).
👁️ Mid-Boss & Rejuvenator 10:00+ • Respawns 5–7m

The central underground raid boss. Can only be damaged by players standing inside the pit.

  • • 50% HP Global Roar: At half health, Mid-Boss screams globally—warning the enemy team to contest or attempt a steal.
  • • Claiming the Crystal: On death, a crystal slowly descends. Heavy Melee it when it lands to claim up to 3 Rejuvenator Revive Credits (3s instant full-HP self-revive + supercharged lane Troopers).
🔓 Walkers & 4 Flex Item Slots Team Progression

Destroying enemy lane structures grants global Souls and unlocks your team's 4 universal Flex Item Slots:

  • • Flex Slot 1: Destroy 3 Enemy Guardians (Tier 1 towers).
  • • Flex Slot 2: Destroy 2 Enemy Walkers (Tier 2 towers).
  • • Flex Slot 3: Destroy All 3 Enemy Walkers.
  • • Flex Slot 4: Destroy 1 Set of Base Guardians (or 1 Shrine).
